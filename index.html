ABOUT TOKSAM

TOKSAM is the project of Brazilian DJ Rafael Zveiter, whose journey in Lisbon’s electronic music scene began in 2017. Driven by groove and the energy of the dance floor, he brings an enthusiastic approach to every set.

His journey includes appearances at Kremlin and the Ferris wheel stage at Rock in Rio Lisboa 2022, alongside sharing line-ups with Nic Fanciulli, Stefano Noferini, Anthony Attalla, Hugo Cantarra, Felguk, Bruno Furlan and DJ Glen.

In late 2022, he launched Toksam & Friends, which evolved into TOKLAB in 2026: an event created to connect Lisbon’s DJs and give artists a platform to come together. Its guests have included Nacho Scoppa. With his sights on dance floors across Europe and Brazil, TOKSAM continues to build his sound and his community.

FAMILIAR FACES. NEW DISCOVERIES.

More than a party.
A place to belong.

What began as Toksam & Friends in late 2022 grew through six editions over two years, including a special guest appearance from Nacho Scoppa. In 2026, the project evolved into TOKLAB, opening a new chapter for its Lisbon community.

Rooted in House and Tech House, with room to explore Techno and beyond, TOKLAB brings experienced DJs and emerging talent together. Through teaching, open decks and carefully curated line-ups, it gives new artists space to develop and a dance floor to connect with.
